## Calls for Lane (1)

1. 1:15, the verb malaq (the priest does this to the bird's head). Not ruled in the pack. Options: **pinch off** (recommended: the rabbis and Milgrom have the priest's fingernail, so a pinch rather than a wring); wring off (WEB); twist off.
2. 1:16, murʾa be-notsatah. Not ruled; Milgrom, Rashi and Ramban disagree. Options: **its crop with its feathers** (recommended: the rabbis' reading, and the Greek has "crop"; Milgrom's different view goes in a note); its tail end by its feathers (Milgrom's crissum, argued with a taxidermist, a minority reading); its crop and its entrails (Rashi's own).
3. Box: Ps 40:6-8 / Heb 10:5-10 (no pleasure in whole burnt offerings; "I have come to do your will"), a reception note at 1:9. Yes (recommended: Hebrews is the lens's top reader of this rite, flagged as reception); no.
4. Box: Eph 5:2 and Rom 12:1 (Christ as fragrant offering; the believer as a living sacrifice), a reception note at 1:9. Yes; **no** (recommended: the Greek of Eph 5:2 is unread; thin without it).
5. Box: Gen 22:3-9 (wood laid in order; Isaac as a going up offering), an echo at 1:7. Yes; **no** (recommended: only the word for laying wood in order is shared; strong in Morales's reading, weak in the text).
6. Box: Mal 1:7-8 (blind and lame animals; "will he accept your person?"), an echo at 1:3. **Yes** (recommended: shares the accepted/acceptance word and the whole-animal demand; Milgrom leans on it); no.

## Decided without Lane

- Pack-ruled renderings applied: cover, going up offering, brought near offering, fire offering, aroma of rest, acceptance/accepted, lean, dash, whole, cut up/pieces. Milgrom's "food gift" and "purge" go in notes only.
- Echoes shipped (strong, shared words): Exod 40:34-35 and 3:4 / 19:3 / 24:16 at 1:1; Exod 29:42-43 at 1:3; Exod 29:15-18 at 1:5-9; Num 8:12 at 1:4; Lev 16:21 at 1:4 (contrast, one hand versus two); Gen 8:20-21 at 1:9; Num 28:2-3 at 1:9; Gen 15:9-10 at 1:17.
- Also shipped, possible: Exod 24:5-8 at 1:5; Lev 9:22-24 at 1:9. Reason: shared words or the same event; cheap, easy to cut.
- Dropped: Exod 25:22 / Num 7:89 (Milgrom's reading of the Priestly view), Exod 12:5, Deut 14:6-7 and Judg 14:6, Judg 19:29 and Ezek 24, Deut 14:4, Exod 40:22 / Num 3:35, Exod 30:12 (kept as Ibn Ezra's view in the verse-by-verse), Gen 8:20 birds (folded into the Gen 8 echo).
- Promotions: none new. The seven tracked threads open here; no further root (tamim, ratson, zaraq) is promoted, since book-wide bias and retrofit cost favour waiting for the units where they recur.
- Fact-check: 67 checked, 6 fixed, 1 unsettled (Greek of Eph 5:2, covered by call 4).
