## Calls for Lane (4)

1. 5:6 and 5:7, ʾasham-wo ("his ʾasham", brought "for his sin"). The ruled "guilt offering" would put two offerings in one verse (the ʾasham and the sin offering). Milgrom reads it as the penalty, not the offering (the same noun as le-ʾashmat at 4:3); the Greek has plēmmeleō (a fault), and Ibn Ezra and Ramban's translator say "guilt offering". Options: **"his penalty"** (recommended: Milgrom and the Greek agree; avoids a second offering; the guilt offering proper starts at 5:14); "his guilt offering" (the ruling, and WEB's "trespass offering"); "his guilt".
2. Box: Heb 13:11-13 (bodies burned outside the camp; Jesus suffered outside the gate), a reception note at 4:12. **Yes** (recommended: Hebrews is the lens's top reader of this rite and the match is exact; flagged as reception, English only); no.
3. Box: Heb 5:1-3, 7:27, 9:7 (the high priest offers for himself and for the people's "ignorances"), a reception note at 4:3. **Yes** (recommended: same reason; the Greek is unread, so keep it as a pointer, not a claim); no.
4. Note on the word "sin offering" at 4:3 (Milgrom's "purification offering": the blood cleans the altar and Tent, not the offerer; Rashi and Ramban differ), moved here from the planned 12:6 and ch. 14. **Yes** (recommended: this is where the argument is made; 12:6 and 14 then point back to it); no, keep it at 12:6.
5. Box: Eccl 5:4-6 (do not delay a vow; do not tell the messenger "it was a mistake", shegagah), an echo at 5:4. Yes; **no** (recommended: the shared word is in a wisdom warning, not a rite; Milgrom cites it only in passing).
