## Calls for Lane (3)

1. 3:3 and throughout, the word heleb (2459), twelve times here. Not ruled in the pack. Options: **fat** (recommended: plain English, matches the ban "eat no fat" at 3:17 and JPS/WEB; the note says it is the peelable fat Milgrom calls suet); suet (Milgrom, precise but an unfamiliar word for the ban).
2. 3:9, ha-ʾalyah. Not ruled. Options: **fat tail** (recommended: reads as it is, WEB, Ellicott, Pulpit); broad tail (Milgrom, JPS).
3. 3:4, kesalim (3689), where the fat on the kidneys sits ("that is on the ..."). Not ruled; Milgrom reports Held's refutation of the usual "loins" (secondhand, unchecked). Options: **sinews** (recommended: the baseline commentator's reading, a note gives "loins" as the older rendering); loins (WEB, JPS, K&D).
4. Promote ha-ḥeleb (2459) to a tracked thread? 48 times in the book, units 3, 4, 6-9, 15, 16; the fat law (3:16-17) is paid off at 7:23-25 and 17:6. **Yes** (recommended: strong book-wide recurrence, low retrofit cost since unit 1 does not use it); no.
5. Promote shelamim (8002) to a tracked thread? 30 times, units 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 16, 18, 21, 22, and a pack-ruled term. **Yes** (recommended: the offering recurs across the book, same as bring-near); no (wait until unit 6 or 8 shows a payoff).
6. Box: Gen 4:4 (Abel brings of his flock "and of its fat"), an echo at 3:3. Yes; **no** (recommended: the shared word is the Hebrew heleb, which this unit can't verify outside Leviticus; thin without it).
7. Box: 1 Cor 10:16-18 (Paul argues from Israel eating the sacrifices: "participants in the altar"), a reception note at 3:1. **Yes** (recommended: Paul uses exactly this meal; flagged as reception, English only); no.
8. Box: Exod 24:5-11 (peace offerings of cattle at the covenant, then "they saw God, and ate and drank"), an echo at 3:1. **Yes** (recommended: the earliest well-being offerings in the Torah, and a meal before God; Milgrom treats it as celebration, not as the covenant itself); no.

## Decided without Lane

- Pack rulings applied: well-being offering, brought near, lean, dash, whole, fire offering, aroma of rest, going up offering. Milgrom's "food gift" goes in a note at 3:11, 3:16 where "food" (lehem) appears; the LXX drops that word.
- 3:5 ʿal ha-ʿolah: "on the going up offering" (literal; the note gives Milgrom's "with the daily going up offering" and Keil and Delitzsch's reading as "over"), because Lev 6:12 (VERIFIED) has the going up offering laid in order on the altar and the fat burned on it after (the order, not "on" the offering itself).
- 3:4 yoteret: "the lobe on the liver" (caudate lobe in a note); 3:3-4 kelayot: kidneys; ha-mekasseh: the fat "that covers".
- Echoes shipped, strong: Lev 1 (threads), Exod 29:13, 22 at 3:3-4 and 3:9, Lev 6:12 at 3:5, Lev 17:5-6 and 7:23-27 at 3:16-17, Lev 9:18-22 at 3:1, 1 Sam 2:13-17 at 3:3, Deut 12:15-16 as a note at 3:17.
- Tentative, footnote only: Ezek 44:7 ("my bread, the fat and the blood") at 3:16; Deut 32:14-15 ("the best", and Jeshurun grown fat) at 3:3. Reason: shared English only, both through Milgrom.
- Dropped: Num 7 and Num 6:14 and Num 28:2, 28:24 (Hebrew opened only as identifiers on the leads sheet); Heb 13:15-16 and Eph 2:13-14 (thin at ch. 3); Gen 8:20-21 (done at 1:9); Judg 6:19-21, 13:15-16; Isa 1:11; Exod 20:24; Deut 14:4.
- Scope: condensed, no verse-by-verse; briefing about 450 words. The open tension (does "in all your dwellings" cover non-sacrificial meat; Milgrom's H-supplement theory against Rashbam and Or HaChaim) stays open in the briefing and the notes.
- Promotions beyond calls 4 and 5: none (organ words stay untracked; they recur only in the lists).
