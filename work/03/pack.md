- 03: condensed. Close variant of 01; difference is what is offered and the fat and blood prohibition (3:16-17).
- 03: The peace offering comes from the herd or the flock, male or female. As with the going up offering, the offerer lays a hand on it and the blood goes on the altar, but only the fat around the inner organs is burned. The chapter ends with a lasting rule: Israel eats no fat and no blood.
