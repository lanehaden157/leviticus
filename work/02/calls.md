## Calls for Lane (2)

1. 2:1, solet (the grade of grain). Not ruled. Options: **fine flour** (recommended: plain and what the English Bibles say; Milgrom's "semolina", wheat grits kept in a note, since his case rests on rabbinic sieve sayings and Akkadian and Arabic cognates, not on the verse); semolina (Milgrom; matches his "groats" for the barley in 2:14-16, which the text sets against flour).
2. 2:2, 'azkarah, with the rare root of "remember". Not ruled. Options: **memorial portion** (recommended: keeps the link to "remember" and to Num 5:26 and 6:15; Milgrom's "token portion" and the fragrance readings of Ibn Ezra and Rashbam go in a note); token portion (Milgrom's provisional pars pro toto); reminder portion.
3. 2:2, same word as a tracked thread (`memorial-portion`, 'azkarah, 6 uses, units 2, 4, 6, 23; no retrofit, unit 1 lacks it). Close call: a thin four-unit root that is also an offering term. Yes, promote (recommended: it reaches the showbread at 24:7 and the jealousy gift; cheap to track); no.
4. 2:12, qorban re'shit. Not ruled. Options: **first-processed offering** (recommended: Milgrom's distinction from bikkurim "first-ripe" in 2:14, backed by Num 18:12-13; the English Bibles flatten both to "firstfruits"); offering of firstfruits (WEB, simplest, loses the 2:12/2:14 contrast); first offering.
5. 2:14, 'abib qaluy ba-'esh geres karmel. Provisional. Options: **green ears parched with fire, groats of the fresh ear** (recommended: Rashi, Rashbam and Ibn Ezra take geres as "broken" grain and karmel as fresh; Milgrom's "milky grain" goes in a note); fresh heads of grain parched with fire and crushed (WEB); new grain, roasted and ground (the Greek's sense, which Milgrom rejects).
6. Box: Exod 30:35 (the holy incense "seasoned with salt"), an echo at 2:13. Tentative: the only other Torah verse with this rare root for salting. Yes (recommended: same sanctuary, same frankincense, one short sentence; easy to cut); no.
7. Box: 1 Cor 5:6-8 and Luke 12:1 (leaven as corruption; "the leaven of the Pharisees"), a reception note at 2:11. Yes (recommended: Milgrom himself cites them; flagged as reception and the Greek is not read this unit); no.
8. Box: Mal 1:11 (a pure gift offering "in every place"), an echo at 2:1. Yes (recommended: rabbis read the verse to mean the cheap gift is as great as an animal; shares the word minhah); no.

## Decided without Lane

- Pack-ruled renderings applied: gift offering (minhah), fire offering, aroma of rest, brought near offering (qorban), turn into smoke. "Food gift" and "cereal offering" stay in notes only.
- Not in the pack and decided by plain sense: nepesh "a person" (Milgrom: neutral for either sex; the same word used in Lev 4-5); hamets "leavened" and se'or "leaven" (Milgrom: se'or leavens the dough, the dough is hamets); debash "honey" with a note (Rashi, Rashbam, Ibn Ezra, Milgrom: fruit or date syrup likely; the bee-honey view in Pliny and Maimonides); qodesh qodashim "most holy"; ha-nnoteret "the remainder"; maḥabat "griddle", marheshet "pan", tannur "oven" (Milgrom, Rashi); hallot "cakes", reqiqim "wafers".
- Promotion: minḥah (4503, 15 uses here, 36 in the book, units 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 13, 22) as a tracked thread, the book's own offering word. Clear, no retrofit (not in unit 1).
- Not promoted: hamets/se'or (4 uses, three units), lebonah (7), melah (3, all here), solet (14, no theme of its own), shemen.
- Echoes shipped (strong, shared words): Lev 5:11-13 and Num 5:15-26 at 2:2; Lev 6:14-18 and Lev 10:12-13 at 2:3 and 2:10; Exod 29:2 and Num 6:15 at 2:4; Exod 12:15 and 13:7 at 2:11; Num 18:19 at 2:13; Lev 23:9-14 at 2:14. Tensions shipped: Lev 7:9-10 against 2:10 (who eats the cooked gift); Lev 7:13 and 23:17 (leaven brought near but not burned) at 2:11-12.
- Also shipped, possible: Josh 5:11 and 2 Kgs 4:42 at 2:14 (parched grain, barley firstfruits). Reason: a rare shared word; cheap and easy to cut.
- Dropped: Gen 4:3-5 (a common word; Abel's was an animal), Jer 7:18 and 2 Kgs 23:9 (Milgrom's reconstruction, no shared words), Matt 5:13 (no sacrificial context), Ezek 43:24 (a point for the notes), 'abib / Exod 13:4 (briefing only).
- Condensed tier: no verse-by-verse (per the unit skill).
- Fact-check: 29 checked, 6 fixed (panel count, Ibn Ezra's "date honey", Or HaChaim's 2:4 comment, the priest's verb in 2:8, Lev 7:10 wording, the briefing's "in every case"), 2 unsettled and neither needs a call: Milgrom's printed "Lev 23:12" for leavened bread (23:12 is the lamb; notes use 23:17), and the pack's three-verse count for we-qamats, not recounted by hand.
