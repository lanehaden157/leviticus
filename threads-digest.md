## Notes per thread

- **`aroma-of-rest`**: A fixed refrain with a Genesis root.
- **`bring-near`**: The book's verb of access; it names the qorban, the brought near offering.
- **`cover`**: The book's central act, from the first chapter to the Day of Coverings.
- **`fat`**: Rendered "fat", the plain word; the notes say it is the fat that can be peeled off, not the fat running through the meat.
- **`fire-offering`**: The book's standard word for what reaches the altar.
- **`gift-offering`**: Rendered "gift offering" so that the wider sense of a gift stays in view; Lev 2 says what this one is made of.
- **`going-up-offering`**: The offering the book keeps returning to: ordinary, ordination, eighth day, Day of Coverings.
- **`lean-the-hand`**: Returns at the blasphemer in the book's last narrative.
- **`reminder-portion`**: A rare word tied to the handful: Lev 2:2, 9, 16; Num 5:26; Lev 6:15; 24:7.
- **`turn-into-smoke`**: A distinct altar verb across the offering and priestly narratives.
- **`well-being-offering`**: Rendered "well-being offering", from shalom; chapter 3 does not say who eats, 7:11–36 does.
