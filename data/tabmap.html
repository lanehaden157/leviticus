To scale from Exod 25-27; where the text gives no position the plan is schematic. In the holiness layer, darker is holier.

  • people: the court, up to the altar (Lev 1:3-5 (the offerer brings it to the entrance and slaughters it). Milgrom reads the limit as the altar (Fig. 8); the verses do not draw a line.)
  • priests: the altar, the basin and the holy place (Exod 28:43; 30:19-20 (they wash at the basin first); Num 18:3-5, 7 (the altar is the priests' charge).)
  • high priest: the holy of holies, once a year (Lev 16:2-3, 16:34; Exod 30:10.)

Not to scale. The Tent stands in the midst of the camp (Num 2:17).

  • east: Judah, Issachar, Zebulun (Num 2:3-9); Moses, Aaron and his sons (Num 3:38)
  • south: Reuben, Simeon, Gad (Num 2:10-16); Kohath (Num 3:29)
  • west: Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin (Num 2:18-24); Gershon (Num 3:23)
  • north: Dan, Asher, Naphtali (Num 2:25-31); Merari (Num 3:35)
