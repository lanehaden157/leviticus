## Tier reasoning (draft)

One line per unit. Full units come from the plan; the rest is a first judgement.

- 01: full. The model offering: the pattern (bring near, lay hand, slaughter, blood, burn) the other offering units refer back to.
- 02: condensed. Short, with little narrative; its key verses (2:1-3, 2:13) carry the grain offering and the salt of the covenant.
- 03: condensed. Close variant of 01; difference is what is offered and the fat and blood prohibition (3:16-17).
- 04: condensed. Four graded cases (priest, congregation, ruler, individual); the key is the repeated pattern and blood placement, which a table shows.
- 05: condensed. Sacred things and neighbour wrong; the key verses are 5:14-16 and 6:1-7 (the fifth added). Heb numbering shifts at 6:1.
- 06: digest. Rubrics for priests and portions; the unit is one subscript-closed block (7:37-38). A summary serves.
- 07: full. Narrative of the ordination, the Exodus 29 pattern; the obedience formula "as Yahweh commanded" returns.
- 08: full. The first sacrifices, the glory and fire (9:23-24); the pair with 10.
- 09: full, peak. The fire that consumes Nadab and Abihu, and its place after 9:24; the book's turning point.
- 10: condensed. A long list; the key verses are the principle (11:44-47). Tables show the animal lists.
- 11: digest. A short case; brings the ḥaṭṭaʾt and ʿolah for the woman (12:6-8).
- 12: condensed. Cases come in a pattern with a verdict formula; tables and strips suit it. Key verses: 13:45-46 and 13:59.
- 13: digest. Cleansing rite in two stages and the house case; the rite is a sequence, so a summary plus a later `ritual` strip.
- 14: digest. The cases are parallel; the closing statement (15:31) is the one verse needed.
- 15: full, peak. The structure of the rite, the two goats, the confession and the sanctuary purged; the book's centre.
- 16: condensed. Blood and slaughter; the pivot is 17:11. Treat as a bridge from 16 into the holiness collection.
- 17: condensed. Tight list; a table of unions and the frame (18:1-5, 24-30).
- 18: full, peak. Holiness for the whole people: the love command (19:18), neighbour, resident alien, mixed cases. Dense with echoes.
- 19: condensed. Matches the wrongs of 18 to penalties; the karet and stoning cases are tabular.
- 20: condensed. The holiness of the high priest and the blemished; key verses are 21:6, 21:10-15.
- 21: digest. Who may eat holy gifts and what is acceptable; ends with the speech-closing refrain (22:31-33).
- 22: condensed. A calendar; ends 23:44. Key verses are the Sabbath (23:3), the Day of Atonement (23:27-32) and Booths.
- 23: full, 24:1-9 condensed. Option chosen: one unit, because the lamp and bread (sacred light and portions) frame the blasphemer story (sacred name) and the unit is read whole. Option B: split into 23a (condensed) and 23b (full, 24:10-23).
- 24: full, peak. Sabbath year and Jubilee; breaks at 25:7 and 25:23 divide the year from the redemption cases.
- 25: full, peak. Redemption cases (land, house, person; the goʾel); the Exodus echo in 25:42, 55. Split from 24 for length (55 verses); one unit if the chat side prefers.
- 26: full, peak. Blessings and curses, the covenant's close; the pair with Deuteronomy 28.
- 27: digest. Appendix on vows and valuations, closed by the subscript (27:34).
