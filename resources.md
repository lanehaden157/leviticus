# Leviticus — Resources

How to weigh each source. Read them through `python tools/kit.py` (keys below;
`kit.py sources` lists what is indexed). Page markers: `[p. N]` printed page
(cite it), `[pdf p. N]` where there is none. Private copies: paraphrase, never
quote at length. Start, not limit: search the web for voices missing here.

## Read whole per unit (`kit.py parts N`)

- **Milgrom**, Leviticus 1-16, 17-22, 23-27 (Anchor Bible 3, 3A, 3B; 1991, 2000, 2001): `milgrom-1/-2/-3`. Baseline. Translation, NOTES verse by verse, long COMMENTS. Strongest on ritual procedure and terms; his ḥaṭṭaʾt-purges-the-sanctuary theory and P/H division drive much of it: say when a reading rests on them. Vol. 2 holds the introduction to H. Transliteration is OCR-damaged: `research/milgrom-transliteration-key.md`. Figures the text lost: `research/figures-milgrom-*.md`.
- **Constable**, Notes on Leviticus (2026): `constable`. Evangelical survey; good outline and NT links, thin on Hebrew.

## By range (`kit.py extra N`)

- **Morales**, Who Shall Ascend the Mountain of the Lord? (NSBT 37, 2015): `morales`. Biblical theology, not commentary: ascent into God's presence, Eden-Sinai-tabernacle as one mountain pattern, the Day of Coverings at the centre. Structure, zones, canon threads; never philology. Figures: `research/figures-morales.md`.
- **Morales**, Exodus Old and New (ESBT, 2020): `morales-exodus`. The exodus pattern through the cult; tabernacle and Sinai.
- **Rashi, Ramban, Ibn Ezra, Sforno** (Sefaria, English): `rashi`, `ramban`, `ibn-ezra`, `sforno`. The medieval Jewish voices: plain sense, midrash behind it (Rashi), theology and Ramban's sanctuary-Sinai link, grammar (Ibn Ezra).
- **Keil & Delitzsch** (1866-): `keil-delitzsch`. Close Hebrew grammar, typology restrained.
- **Ellicott** (Ginsburg on Leviticus, 1880s): `ellicott`. Ginsburg brings rabbinic tradition and Second Temple practice.
- **Pulpit** (exposition only): `pulpit`. **Henry**: `henry`. Older Christian reading; devotion light.
- **JPS** gender-sensitive translation (Sefaria): `jps`. A second English, with notes. **Tyndale** tagged Hebrew (CC BY): `tyndale`. Word glosses to set beside `kit.py words`.

## Texts

`kit.py words <range>`: each verse's English (WEB), words (transliteration, Strong's lemma, gloss, parsing) and Brenton's LXX English. `kit.py bible <CODE> <range> [--lxx]`: any book. `python -m biblecore lxx <Book> <C:V>`: LXX Greek lemmas.
