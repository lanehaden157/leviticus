## Ch. 1 — Leviticus within the Pentateuch

### 1. pdf p. 34 — Pentateuch as A-B-C-B'-A' (table, Leder)
- Passages: Gen–Deut.
- Shows: the five books as a concentric chart with Leviticus alone at the centre.
- Structure: A Genesis and A' Deuteronomy = separation from the nations, blessing, seeing the land, descendants and land. B Exodus and B' Numbers = desert journeys, apostasy and plagues, firstborn/Levites (Exodus adds Pharaoh and the magicians; Numbers adds Balak and Balaam). C Leviticus = sacrifices, cleanness, holiness.
- Feeds: canon thread (Leviticus as centre of the Torah); intro/unit map framing.
- Uncertain: none. Table is clean in the PDF (text layout lost only in the txt).

### 2. pdf p. 35 — Kline's Pentateuch schematic (table)
- Passages: Gen–Deut.
- Shows: a stepped list, each book indented a step further to a peak at Leviticus.
- Structure: Genesis prologue; Exodus leaving Egypt and building the tabernacle; Leviticus the tabernacle service; Numbers dedicating the tabernacle and preparing to enter Canaan; Deuteronomy epilogue. The tabernacle runs through Exodus–Numbers, with Leviticus as its service.
- Feeds: canon thread; tabernacle map (tabernacle-centred reading of the Torah).
- Uncertain: attributed in his text to Kline (2006); the printed layout is a plain table.

### 3. pdf p. 35 — Wenham's journey-stop zigzag (figure, drawn)
- Passages: Exod 1 – Num 36.
- Shows: a line dropping and rising between stations.
- Structure: Egypt → (Exod 13) → Sinai (Exod 19 to Num 10, containing Leviticus) → (Num 13) → Kadesh → (Num 20–22) → Plains of Moab (Num 36). Three journey-stop cycles. Sinai is the first long stop.
- Feeds: unit map (position of Leviticus in the itinerary); Wave 2 "where" strip for the camp.
- Uncertain: chapter numbers on the line are approximate (13, 19; 10, 13; 20, 22, 36).

### 4. pdf p. 35–36 — Schart's ring, Exod 15:22 – Num 21:18 (indented text, set as a table)
- Shows: a symmetrical pattern with Sinai as the axis. The converter split it across two pages.
- Structure: A Exod 15:22–25 bitter→sweet water; B 17:1–7 water from rock; C 17:8–16 Amalekite war; D 18 leadership relief; E 18:27 Hobab; F 19:1–2 arrival at Sinai. [SINAI]. F' Num 10:11–23 departure; E' 10:29–32 Hobab; D' 11 leadership relief; C' 14:39–45 Amalekite war; B' 20:1–13 water from rock; A' 21:16–18 the spring.
- Feeds: canon thread / pentateuch frame; Exodus↔Numbers pairs.
- Uncertain: none. (Counts as one entry across two placeholders.)

### 5. pdf p. 36 — Date-notice bracket of Leviticus (indented text, table placeholder)
- Passages: Exod 40:17; Num 1:1.
- Shows: two date notices framing the book.
- Structure: G Exod 40:17 = 1st day, 1st month, year 2: "tabernacle raised". [Sinai: Leviticus]. G' Num 1:1 = 1st day, 2nd month, year 2: "tent of meeting". Leviticus fills the one month between.
- Feeds: unit map (book-level frame/timeline); calendar.
- Uncertain: none.

### 6. pdf p. 38 — Davidson/Shea pyramid of the Pentateuch (figure, drawn)
- Passages: Gen–Deut; Lev 1–27.
- Shows: a triangle of horizontal bands, outer pairs at the base, Leviticus 16 at the apex. Side brackets labelled "Blood" (ascending) and "Holiness" (descending).
- Structure: A Genesis / A' Deuteronomy; B Exodus / B' Numbers; Leviticus as the next level: C 1–7 sanctuary laws / C' 23–27; D 8–10 priestly laws / D' 21–22; E 11–15 personal laws / E' 17–20; F 16 Day of Atonement at the peak.
- Feeds: unit map / peaks (apex = Lev 16); chiasm proposal for Leviticus.
- Uncertain: Morales says he altered Davidson's diagram slightly. Note he puts 17 into E' (17–20) while his own outline (item 7) lists 17–20 and 16 apart.

### 7. pdf p. 39 — Morales's sevenfold outline of Leviticus (stepped text, not a placeholder)
- Shows: seven blocks stepped up to ch. 16 and down, with a two-arrow label (approach / communion).
- Structure: 1–7 sacrifices; 8–10 priesthood instituted, cult inaugurated; 11–15 clean/unclean in daily life; 16 Day of Atonement; 17–20 holy/profane in daily life; 21–22 priestly legislation; 23–27 festivals/sacred time. First half: approaching God (atonement, judgment/cleansing); second: communion with God (holiness).
- Feeds: unit map / peaks; Leviticus overview. See outline at end.
- Uncertain: the text layout is garbled; labels read from the page image.

### 8. pdf p. 47 — Pentateuch as a journey up Sinai (figure, drawn)
- Passages: Exod 18:27 – Num 10:11; Lev 16.
- Shows: a sketch of a smoking mountain with the tabernacle at its summit, and arrows rising from the wilderness on the left and leaving on the right.
- Structure: approach = Exod 18:27 wilderness journey; mountain base = "Exod 19:1 – Mount Sinai – Num 10:10"; summit = tabernacle (= Leviticus); cloud/smoke above = Day of Atonement; exit = Num 10:11 wilderness journey.
- Feeds: canon thread (mountain of God); peaks (Lev 16 as the summit).
- Uncertain: none.

## Ch. 2 — Longing for Eden (Genesis)

### 9. pdf p. 55 — Creation days as habitation / time / inhabitants (figure, text diagram)
- Passages: Gen 1:1–2:3.
- Shows: three columns pairing days.
- Structure: time column = day 1 (day and night), day 4 (annual cultic festivals), day 7 (weekly Sabbath). Habitation→inhabitants pairs: day 2 sky/water → day 5 fish/fowl; day 3 land/plants → day 6 animals/humans. Day 4 is the centre.
- Feeds: calendar (festival time rooted in creation); canon thread (sacred time).
- Uncertain: none.

### 10. pdf p. 55 — Gen 1:14–18 palistrophe (indented text, not a placeholder)
- Structure: A divide day from night (14a); B signs, "cultic festivals", days and years (14b); C give light on the earth (15); D rule the day (16a) / D' rule the night (16b) (the two lamps); C' give light on the earth (17); the pattern continues to the end of v. 18 (partly lost on the page).
- Feeds: calendar (moʿadim in creation).
- Uncertain: B/C/D labels read from the image; v. 18 closing member not captured.

### 11. pdf p. 61 — Cosmos with the mountain of God (figure, drawn)
- Passages: Ps 18; Gen 2; Ezek 28 (context).
- Shows: a hand-drawn world-picture. Heavens/waters above, a central mountain with the divine dwelling and clouds, the sea/Tehom below, pillars, sea monsters, Sheol.
- Structure: top to bottom = raqia / mabbul; mountain of God with sun and sky; encircling land; Tehom and sea creatures; Sheol. Labelled "Cosmos".
- Feeds: canon thread (Eden/mountain cosmology); tabernacle map (cosmos = cult; see #17).
- Uncertain: labels in Hebrew transliteration; text beside it is Ps 18 as a pattern of descent and rescue.

### 12. pdf p. 80 — Universal history: Eden and Ararat (figure, drawn)
- Passages: Gen 1–11.
- Shows: two parallel rises. Beginning: Tehom (Sheol) → Eden (Adam) → Cain's city. New beginning after "End": Deluge (Sheol) → Ararat (Noah) → Tower of Babylon.
- Feeds: canon thread (mountain / city-of-man pattern); type-scene proposal (flood = Sheol; ark as sanctuary).
- Uncertain: none. Icon pattern: waters → mountain → city.

### 13. pdf p. 84 — Abraham: Ur, Moriah, Ai (figure, drawn)
- Passages: Gen 12–22.
- Shows: the same icon for Abraham. Ur (Sheol) → Moriah/altar → city of Ai; it extends Miller's three pairs: Eden–Cain's city, Noah's ark–Babel, Abram's altar–Sodom.

## Ch. 5 — Cleansing the house of God (Lev 11–16)

### 18. pdf p. 180 — Holy / common / clean / unclean boundaries (table, after Wenham)
- Passages: Lev 10:10; 11–15; 15:31; 16:19.
- Shows: a three-band chart with two boundaries (holy|common and clean|unclean), with arrows for the forces crossing each.
- Structure: Holy | Common (clean, unclean). Sacrifice acts on the whole system. Sanctify = clean → holy. Cleanse = unclean → clean. Profane = holy → common. Pollute = clean → unclean. Sin or infirmity pushes the wrong way. Holy and unclean must never meet. Holy/common concerns status; clean/unclean concerns condition.
- Feeds: **ritual** (what each rite does: cleanse vs sanctify); tabernacle map "who may enter" layer; condensed table for Lev 11–15.
- Uncertain: Morales says "based on Wenham's"; the arrows are his rendering.

### 19. pdf p. 190 — Cosmos and cultic cosmos: three grades (table)
- Passages: Lev 10:1–3; 11–16; Num 1–4.
- Shows: an upward arrow beside two parallel lists: "Cosmos" (humanity in relation to YHWH) and "Cultic cosmos" (animals, space).
- Structure: Holy = priests = sacrificial animals = tabernacle. Clean = Israel = clean animals = camp. Unclean = Gentiles = unclean animals = wilderness. Movement runs upward toward YHWH. He ties it to Nadab and Abihu (before 11–15) and Aaron in the holy of holies (after).
- Feeds: **tabernacle map** (zones outside camp / camp / sanctuary, and the "who may enter" layer); ritual (approach as stepped movement); lawparallel for animal grades.
- Uncertain: his own schematic. It puts court and holy place together as "tabernacle", so do not use it for finer zones.

### 20. pdf p. 205 — Exile eastward / Exodus westward: Eden and tabernacle (figure, drawn)
- Passages: Gen 2–3; Exod 25–40; Lev 16.
- Shows: two panels. Top: Eden (tree of life, other tree, cherubim and flaming sword, four rivers, mountain of the Lord) with a black arrow "Expulsion" east. Bottom: a cutaway tabernacle with a black arrow "Atonement" back west.
- Structure: tabernacle, inner to outer: holy of holies (cherubim on the mercy seat over the ark; cherubim on the veil) → holy place (menorah; cherubim on the curtain) → court (bronze altar, bronze laver). The menorah matches the tree of life; cherubim guard both.
- Feeds: **tabernacle map** (inner-to-outer order, furniture by zone); canon thread (Eden → tabernacle).
- Uncertain: a cutaway, not to scale; the laver and altar are drawn side by side outside the tent.

### 21. pdf p. 214 — Recap of the mountain icons (figure, drawn, composite)
- Passages: Gen 1–11; 12–22; Exod 19–40.
- Shows: Eden, Ararat, Moriah/Zion, Sinai and tabernacle-cultus icons, in two rows.
- Feeds: canon thread. Repeats #12, #13, #15, #16; no new facts.

## Ch. 6 — Meeting with God at the house of God (Lev 17–27)

### 22. pdf p. 235 — Sabbath-word density across Leviticus (figure, chart)
- Passages: Lev 1–27 (the root for Sabbath).
- Shows: an area graph of hits per 1,000 words, chapter by chapter.
- Structure (read by eye, approximate): zero through ch. 16; about 1 from ch. 16/17 to 20 (about 2 near 19–20); zero at about ch. 22; steep rise to about 10 around ch. 23–25; a dip near the 25/26 boundary; a second peak of about 8 in ch. 26; about 1 at ch. 27.
- Feeds: calendar and unit map (Sabbath and sacred time cluster in 23–26); peaks.
- Uncertain: values are estimates from the picture. His point: Leviticus ends on sacred time and the Sabbath. Don't publish the numbers.

### 23. pdf p. 238 — Crisis → resolution through Lev 9–26 (table)
- Passages: Exod 40:35; Lev 1–26.
- Shows: a grid (crisis / inserted legislation / resolution / divine presence), one row per movement and a covenant row at the foot. The four arrows after it (p. 239) restate it.
- Structure: Row 1 Exod 40 – Lev 9–10: no entry (Exod 40:35) → sacrifices and priesthood (Lev 1–8) → entry (Lev 9) → public theophany on the eighth day (9:23). Row 2 Lev 11–16: tabernacle polluted, limits of approach (10:1–3) → clean/unclean laws (11–15) → tabernacle cleansed, YHWH approached in the inner sanctum (16) → theophany there on the Day of Atonement (16:2). Row 3 Lev 17–23/25: second response to Nadab and Abihu (10:1–3) → holy/profane laws (17–22) → Sabbath assemblies at God's house (23–25) → theophany in the holy place on the Sabbath (24:1–9). Row 4 Lev 26: promises and threats (Presence like Eden, 26:12; or exile, 26:33). Arrows: (1) tabernacle, Exod 25–31 and 35–40; (2) sacrifices 1–7, priesthood 8; (3) clean/unclean 11–15 → 16; (4) holy/profane 17–22 → 23–25.
- Feeds: unit map / peaks (sections as a drama of crisis and resolution); ritual (entry sequence); canon thread.
- Uncertain: his own reading; the four-stage cut differs from his sevenfold outline (#7).

### 24. pdf p. 245 — Lev 18 vs Lev 20 prohibitions (table; not a placeholder)
- Passages: Lev 18:6–30; 20:2–22.
- Shows: two columns lining up each offence in the two chapters, each ending with the land-vomiting warning.
- Structure: incest 18:16–18 / 20:11–21; menstrual 18:19 / 20:18; adultery 18:20 / 20:10; Molech 18:21 / 20:2–5; same-sex 18:22 / 20:13; bestiality 18:23 / 20:15–16. Only in 20: mediums 20:6–8; cursing parents 20:9. Closing frames 18:24–30 and 20:22. He reads them (after Douglas) as breaches of covenant tied to Egypt/Canaan cults, not family law.
- Feeds: **lawparallel** (Lev 18 ↔ 20 pairing).
- Uncertain: his verse ranges are selective (18:6–15 not listed). Rebuild from the text rather than copy.
